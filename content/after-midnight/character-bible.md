# AFTER MIDNIGHT — Character Bible

## Malik Reed

**Age:** 26  
**Role:** Overnight radio host  
**Function:** Protagonist

### Core personality
- Sharp and observant.
- Calm under pressure because listeners depend on him.
- Funny when nervous, but never goofy.
- Curious enough to investigate things that should be left alone.
- His confidence comes from knowing his environment; the mystery works because that confidence slowly breaks.

### Performance direction
Malik should feel like a real overnight radio host, not an exaggerated horror protagonist. Dialogue is conversational and understated. Fear appears first through pauses, eye movement, breathing, and small changes in posture.

### Visual continuity
- Contemporary casual overnight-shift wardrobe.
- Dark neutral hoodie or jacket over a simple shirt.
- Natural hair texture and realistic skin detail.
- No unnecessary jewelry or flashy accessories.
- Maintain the same facial structure, hairstyle, wardrobe palette and proportions across every shot.

### Screen language
When Malik is confident, use stable framing and controlled movement. As reality becomes uncertain, introduce slightly tighter framing and restrained handheld movement. Avoid excessive horror effects.

## The Woman

**Role:** Anonymous caller from Bellweather  
**Function:** Mystery catalyst

She is heard but not visually revealed in Episode 1. Her voice should be calm, direct and familiar enough to make Malik uncomfortable without sounding theatrical.

## Continuity rules
1. Never redesign Malik between shots.
2. Keep the radio station layout consistent.
3. Keep the same telephone and ON AIR light throughout the episode.
4. Supernatural details remain subtle.
5. Prioritize believable human performance over visual spectacle.
